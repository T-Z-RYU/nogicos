## 一、YC 叙事方案

### 1.1 One-liner（最终版）

**Workspace AI connects your tabs, files, and apps**

(48 characters)

### 1.2 品类定位

| 维度 | 定位 |
|------|------|
| **品类** | Workspace AI（工作环境 AI）|
| **核心动词** | connects（连接）|
| **范围** | tabs, files, and apps |
| **差异化** | 跟 ramAIn（通讯自动化）不同，我们做研究/分析 |

### 1.3 核心叙事（1 分钟 Pitch）

1. **问题：** 知识工作者的工作分散在浏览器标签页、本地文件、各种应用里。现有 AI 只能看到一个窗口。

2. **洞察：** 这不是 AI 能力的问题，是上下文**连接**的问题。Cursor 解决了程序员的这个问题。但知识工作者呢？

3. **解决方案：** NogicOS 是一个 Workspace AI，能连接你的 tabs、files 和 apps。它不只是聊天，它能直接在你的工作环境里行动。

4. **差异化：** ramAIn 做通讯自动化（WhatsApp/Email）。我们做研究和分析——竞品分析、市场调研、文档整合。不同用户，不同场景。

5. **愿景：** 让每一个知识工作者都有一个 AI 伙伴，它能连接你的完整工作环境，直接帮你行动。

### 1.4 叙事层次

```
Level 1 - 功能层：
  "AI that connects your tabs, files, and apps"
  
Level 2 - 价值层：
  "AI that understands your complete work context"
  
Level 3 - 差异化：
  "Research & analysis, not communication automation"
  
Level 4 - 品类定位：
  "Workspace AI - a new category"
```

## 五、YC 申请表填写参考

### 5.1 公司描述（Describe what your company does in 50 characters or less）

> **Workspace AI connects your tabs, files, and apps**
> (48 characters)

### 5.2 一句话扩展（One sentence description）

> NogicOS is a Workspace AI that connects your browser tabs, local files, and desktop apps, helping knowledge workers do research and analysis without copy-pasting context.

### 5.3 问题（What problem are you solving?）

> Knowledge workers spend 80% of their time switching between browser tabs, local files, and desktop apps. Every time they ask AI for help, they waste 5 minutes explaining context. Cursor solved this for developers. ramAIn solved it for messaging. We're solving it for research and analysis.

### 5.4 解决方案（How do you solve it?）

> NogicOS connects browser control (Playwright), local file access, and desktop understanding into a single AI agent. It can navigate websites, read/write files, and understand your screen—helping users do competitive analysis, market research, and document integration across platforms.

### 5.5 差异化（What makes you different?）

> 1. **Workspace AI**: New category—we connect tabs, files, and apps
> 2. **Research Focus**: ramAIn does messaging automation. We do research and analysis.
> 3. **Knowledge Workers**: PMs, analysts, researchers—not just business communication
> 4. **Direct Action**: Not just chat—executes tasks across your workspace

### 5.6 如果 YC 问「你跟 ramAIn 有什么不同」

> "ramAIn automates messaging—WhatsApp, email. We automate research—browsing, reading files, competitive analysis. Different users, different use cases. They're an AI messaging assistant. We're a Workspace AI for knowledge workers."
